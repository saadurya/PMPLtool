# Problem

Product Managers often start with a product problem, user signal, business concern, or assumption but may not know what to investigate next.

The challenge is not simply finding a solution.

The challenge is determining:

- What is actually happening?
- What do we know?
- What are we assuming?
- What could be causing the problem?
- What evidence do we need?
- Which hypothesis should we investigate first?
- Do we need user research, data analysis, competitor analysis, experimentation, or something else?
- What should the PM do next?

PMPLtool aims to guide a Product Manager from an initial problem toward an evidence-backed product direction.

The tool should help separate:

- Facts
- Signals
- Assumptions
- Hypotheses
- Evidence
- Unknowns
- Possible root causes
- Priorities
- Recommended next steps

The goal is not to blindly accept the user's assumptions.

The goal is to help the PM investigate the problem and reach a better-informed product decision.
