# Product Decisions

This document records the important decisions made while building PMPLtool.

The purpose is to document the product thinking behind the project, not just the final implementation.

---

## Day 0 — Project Direction

### Decision

Build PMPLtool as a guided Product Management problem-solving and learning tool.

### Why

I want to build a small software project that helps me learn and practice Product Management while creating something useful for PMs.

Rather than building a collection of unrelated PM calculators, PMPLtool will connect different Product Management concepts into a guided problem-solving journey.

### Initial direction

Problem
→ Investigation
→ Evidence
→ Root Causes
→ Hypotheses
→ Prioritization
→ Next Steps
→ Solution Direction
→ Product Roadmap

### Core product principle

> The problem determines the roadmap.

Different product problems should lead to different recommended next steps.

For example:

- A conversion problem may require funnel and metrics analysis.
- A user satisfaction problem may require user research.
- A feature request may require demand validation and competitor analysis.
- A technical issue may require engineering investigation.
- A new product opportunity may require market and competitor research.

### Important principle

The tool should not blindly accept the user's assumptions.

User-provided information should be classified as appropriate:

- Fact
- Signal
- Assumption
- Hypothesis
- Unknown

The tool should help the user understand what evidence is needed to confirm or challenge their thinking.

### Learning objective

PMPLtool is also a learning project.

The development journey should help me practice and understand Product Management concepts including:

- Problem discovery
- User research
- Root-cause analysis
- Product analytics
- Metrics
- North Star Metrics
- Funnels
- Prioritization frameworks
- Hypothesis-driven development
- Experimentation
- Competitor analysis
- Prototyping
- PRDs
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Product roadmaps
- Product strategy
- Launch and measurement
- Iteration

### Future direction

Explore ways to use reliable external product, market, user-review, and other relevant data to validate or challenge assumptions.

The tool should prioritize evidence over assumptions.
